🏕️ Welcome, Scouts!

Get ready for an adventure! Scattered around the area are NFC tags — each one holds a clue that leads you to the next station. Work together, use your Scout skills, and see if your den can find them all!

🗺️   Multiple Stations to Find!

📋 How to Play

  1. 1 Ask your den leader for your group's unique starting link.
  2. 2 Tap your phone or device to an NFC tag when you find one.
  3. 3 Read the clue on the page and figure out where to go next.
  4. 4 Complete the activity or answer the question at each station.
  5. 5 Find all the stations and report back to your den leader!

⚡ Scout Tips

🔦 Stay together — Always hunt in pairs or as a group. No Scout hunts alone!
🧭 Be Prepared — Keep your eyes open for anything that looks out of place.
🤝 Help each other — If your den is stuck, put your heads together and brainstorm!

🚀 Your Starting Station

Ask your den leader for your group's starting link, then tap the NFC tag at your first station to begin!
